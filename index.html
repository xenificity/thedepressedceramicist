The work

Out of the mold

Everything is slipcast by hand in the studio: liquid clay poured into plaster molds, then trimmed, fired and glazed one piece at a time.

Paint your own pottery

Walk in any time during open hours, pick a piece and paint. Pieces start at $5, with the most to choose from in the $15–25 range.

Classes

Regular classes at the studio. Call or message on Facebook to find out what's coming up.

Bisque to take home

Slipcast pieces, astrology steins included, are available in bisque so you can paint them your way. Send a photo of the finished piece!

Gatherings

Why have "bored" meetings when you can have arty parties?

Birthday parties, bridal showers, team building, field trips and girls' nights. Tell us your vision and we'll handle the rest, from decorations to party food. Best part: no clean-up for you.

Evening and weekend times are available for private parties and special events.

About

Hi, I'm India.

I'm India McCullough, owner of The Art Room, the arts and crafts studio at 304 West Main Street in Sedalia. It opened in 2020 with monthly classes and has grown into a studio that's open five days a week.

The Depressed Ceramicist is where I share my slipcasting: pouring liquid clay into molds, opening them up, and showing what comes out, along with some honest talk about mental health. Come by and see the pieces in person.

Have ceramic slip molds you don't need anymore? I'm always interested in buying them.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need an appointment to come paint?

No! Come paint any time during open hours. Wednesday and Thursday are by appointment.

How much does it cost?

Pieces start at $5. Most of the selection is in the $15–25 range.

Can I host a party or event?

Yes: birthdays, showers, team building, field trips and more. Email events@artroomusa.com or message The Art Room on Facebook.

Can I get a refund on a class?

Classes, events and lessons are non-refundable unless management agrees otherwise.

Do you buy ceramic slip molds?

Yes, we're interested!
