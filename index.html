The work

Out of the mold

Everything is slipcast by hand in the studio: liquid clay poured into plaster molds, then trimmed, fired and glazed one piece at a time.

Finished pieces

Glazed and fired, ready to take home. Stock changes all the time, so check social media for what's new.

Bisque to paint yourself

Want to glaze it your way? Many pieces come in bisque so you can finish them at your local paint-your-own pottery shop.

Astrology steins

A stein for every sign. Ask about yours, and send a photo of the finished piece if you painted it!

About

Hi, I'm India.

The Depressed Ceramicist started as a way to keep my hands busy on the hard days, and turned into a studio, a storefront on West Main Street, and a community of hundreds of thousands of people who show up for clay videos and honest talk about mental health.

Slipcasting is my favorite part of the process. Pouring, waiting, and opening a mold is a little like weather: you never quite know what you'll get, but it's usually worth it.
