The work

Out of the mold.

Everything is slipcast in the studio. Liquid clay goes into plaster molds, and each piece is trimmed, fired and glazed one at a time. The astrology steins are a favorite: a stein for every sign, sold finished or in bisque so you can paint your own.

  1. 01Pour liquid slip into a plaster mold.
  2. 02Wait while the plaster pulls water and a clay wall forms.
  3. 03Release the piece, then trim and clean every seam.
  4. 04Fire & glaze, or keep it in bisque for you to paint.

Meet the ceramicist

"I love slipcasting AND I'm depressed."

India McCullough owns The Art Room, the arts and crafts studio at 304 West Main Street in Sedalia. It opened in 2020 with monthly classes and has grown into a studio that's open five days a week.

As The Depressed Ceramicist, India shares slipcasting with hundreds of thousands of followers: pouring molds, opening them up and showing what comes out, along with some honest talk about mental health.

FAQ

Do I need an appointment to come paint?

No! Come paint any time during open hours. Wednesday and Thursday are by appointment.

Pieces start at $5. Most of the selection is in the $15–25 range.

Yes: birthdays, showers, team building, field trips and more. Email events@artroomusa.com or message The Art Room on Facebook.

Classes, events and lessons are non-refundable unless management agrees otherwise.
